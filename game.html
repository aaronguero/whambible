Tutorial

PAPA

Welcome, warrior! Let me show you how to battle with the Word. It's simple — and you'll be a Champion before you know it.

How the Game Works
📜
Read the Verse

A Bible verse appears on screen. Your mission: identify exactly where it's found in Scripture.

🎯
Choose the Location

Four options appear — each showing a Book Chapter Verse. Tap the correct one before time runs out!

⏱️
Beat the Clock

You have 20 seconds per verse. The gold timer bar shrinks — then turns red. Don't overthink it!

Scoring
✅
Correct Answer

You earn points based on your chosen level. Chain correct answers to build your streak!

🗡️ Squire — 5 pts ⚔️ Warrior — 10 pts 🛡️ Knight — 15 pts 👑 Champion — 20 pts
🔥
Streak Bonus

String together correct answers without missing and earn bonus points on top of your base score. Keep the fire burning!

3 in a row → +3 pts 5 in a row → +5 pts
❌
Wrong Answer

No points lost — but your streak resets. You'll get a chance to recover!

0 pts · streak reset
Scroll Recovery
📿
The Recovery Scroll

Miss a verse? A recovery scroll appears. Spin the wheels to land on the correct Book · Chapter · Verse within 7 seconds to earn recovery points!

Successful recovery → +5 pts
Multiplayer
👥
Challenge Friends

Find a player and send them a challenge verse. They answer on their device — you get notified. Play async, anytime.

🏆
Climb the Ranks

Earn points across games to rise through the ranks:

📜 Scribe — 0 🗡️ Squire — 1+ ⚔️ Warrior — 100+ 🛡️ Knight — 300+ 👑 Champion — 700+
