1. Program Description

WhamBible Game Alerts is a free opt-in SMS notification program. When you provide your phone number and check the SMS consent box in the WhamBible app, you will receive automated text message notifications including:

  • Notifications when another player challenges you to a verse battle.
  • Notifications when it is your turn to answer or pick a verse in an active multiplayer game.
  • Notifications when a game result is posted.

These are transactional game-play notifications. WhamBible does not send promotional or marketing SMS messages.

2. How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS alerts by:

  1. Creating a WhamBible account at whambible.com.
  2. Entering your mobile phone number in the optional phone field.
  3. Checking the SMS consent checkbox which states: "By checking this box, I agree to receive automated SMS game alert messages from WhamBible at the number above. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase or use."

You may also enable or disable SMS alerts at any time from your My Profile screen within the WhamBible app.

Providing a phone number and enrolling in SMS alerts is entirely optional and is never required to play WhamBible.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your game activity. You will only receive messages when you have an active multiplayer game or when another player initiates a challenge with you. During periods of inactivity, you will receive no messages.

4. Charges & Costs

WhamBible does not charge for SMS alerts. However, message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan. Contact your carrier for details on your plan's messaging rates.

5. How to Opt Out (STOP)

To stop receiving SMS messages from WhamBible at any time, reply STOP to any message we send you.

After replying STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. You can re-enable alerts at any time from your profile in the app.

Recognized opt-out keywords: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT

6. How to Get Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message for support information, or contact us at support@whambible.com.

When you reply HELP, you will receive a message with our support contact information.
