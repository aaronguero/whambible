How to Play

⚔️
Issue a Challenge

Find an opponent and send them a challenge. You are the Challenger for the whole game.

🎯
Pick a Level

Choose 5, 10, 15, or 20 points. Harder levels use rarer verses — and pay out more!

📖
Answer the Verse

Your opponent sees the verse and picks the correct Book, Chapter, and Verse from 4 options.

🌀
Scroll Recovery

Miss an answer? Spin the recovery scroll within 7 seconds to still earn 5 bonus points.

🏆
10 Rounds — Best Score Wins

After 10 rounds the player with the most points claims victory!
