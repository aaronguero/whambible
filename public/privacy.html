Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 24, 2026  ·  Last Updated: April 24, 2026

WhamBible ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you use WhamBible at whambible.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Display name and email address when you create an account.
  • Phone Number (Optional): Provided voluntarily only if you choose to receive SMS game alert notifications. A phone number is never required to play.
  • Game Data: Score history, games played, wins, losses, and Bible verse interaction data to power leaderboards and rank progression.
  • Device & Usage Data: Browser type, device type, and general usage patterns via standard server logs. We do not use third-party tracking SDKs or advertising cookies.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the WhamBible game experience.
  • To maintain your profile, scores, and rank across sessions.
  • To send SMS game alert notifications only if you explicitly opt in and provide your phone number.
  • To display leaderboards using display names only — your email and phone number are never shown publicly.
  • To respond to support requests.

3. SMS Messaging & Text Message Program

⚠️  We do not share your SMS opt-in data or phone number with any third parties for marketing purposes. All SMS opt-in data and consent information will not be shared with any third parties, excluding aggregators and providers of the text messaging services used to deliver your messages.

If you provide your phone number and check the SMS consent box during signup, you agree to receive automated game alert text messages from WhamBible. Message frequency varies based on your game activity. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for support. You may also toggle SMS alerts on or off at any time from your My Profile screen within the app.

See our full SMS Terms & Conditions for complete program details.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Limited sharing occurs only in these circumstances:

  • SMS Delivery (Twilio): If you opt in to SMS, your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely to deliver your message. Twilio does not use your data for marketing. See Twilio's Privacy Policy.
  • Infrastructure Providers: We use Netlify for hosting and Base44 for backend data storage. These providers process data on our behalf under confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account is active. Upon request, we will delete your personal data within 30 days. Aggregated non-identifiable statistics may be retained for platform improvement.

6. Security

We use industry-standard practices including HTTPS encryption and secure API tokens. No transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security but take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

7. Children's Privacy

WhamBible is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated personal data.
  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message or toggling off in your profile.

To exercise these rights: support@whambible.com

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of WhamBible after changes constitutes acceptance.
